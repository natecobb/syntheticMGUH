This 45-year-old female with a history of essential hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus presents for evaluation of subacute abdominal pain of two days' duration, without associated emesis or pyrexia.
Physical examination reveals a soft, non-distended abdomen with localized right lower quadrant tenderness to palpation and absence of rebound or involuntary guarding.
Diagnostic considerations include acute gastritis versus early appendicitis. Initiated esomeprazole 40 mg daily with scheduled reassessment in 48 hours and explicit emergency department precautions.
